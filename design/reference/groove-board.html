Wobble design direction 1 of 3

Groove

A dark instrument console. The mark is the thing Wobble is named after: the pregroove of a recordable disc, which wobbles so the drive can find its place. The colour is the spectrum a disc throws back, used only where something is live.

The icon

One ring that wobbles, on a dark tile, in the violet to cyan to pink a disc reflects. The inner ring and the hub give it depth at large sizes and drop away below 32 pixels.

Every size it ships at

32 pixels and below use a heavier drawing with fewer parts, so the shape holds in the taskbar and the tray.

WobbleA CD, DVD and Blu-ray drive emulator for testing Windows optical disc software.

Tray icon states

A badge on the mark says what the emulated drive is doing without opening the window.

Media glyphs

One colour per family, a dashed outline for an empty tray, an arc for rewritable.

The control window

Three columns that never move: the disc on the left, the command trace in the middle where the eye stays, fault rules on the right. Dense, monospaced where bytes are shown, built for a second monitor. The data is an example: a raw CD burn from a cue sheet with one fault rule that has just fired.
